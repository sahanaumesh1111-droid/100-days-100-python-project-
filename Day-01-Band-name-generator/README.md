# Day 1 - Band Name Generator 🎸

## About
A simple Python project that generates a band name using your city and pet's name.

## How it works
1. Enter the city where you grew up.
2. Enter your pet's name.
3. Get your band name!

## Sample Output
```text
Which city did you grow up in? Mysore
What was your pet's name? Bruno
Your band name could be Mysore Bruno
```
